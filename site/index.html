You stay in charge.

An assistant that can click anything needs clear rules. These are Marigold's.

  • It asks before sending, buying or deleting, once per kind of action.
  • Anything involving money asks every single time, with Touch ID.
  • It never types your passwords. You do that yourself.
  • Your chats and files stay on your Mac. Only your questions go to the AI service.
  • One click stops everything, and a full log shows every action it took.

Questions

The short answers.

Which Macs does it run on?
Any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs aren't supported yet.
What does it cost?
Marigold is free while we test. Pricing will be announced before anything is charged, and you'll never be billed without asking.
macOS says it can't verify the app. Is that safe?
Test builds aren't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS adds one extra step. Right-click Marigold in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still blocks it, open Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Marigold.app, then open it again. This goes away once Marigold is notarized.
Does it see my passwords, texts or files?
It can read what you let it: apps need your permission, texts need Full Disk Access you grant yourself, and it never types passwords or card numbers. Everything it does is logged in Settings, where you can revoke anything it remembered.
Where do my questions go?
Your chats and settings stay on your Mac. The questions you ask are sent to the AI service Marigold uses to answer them, and searches go to the public sources listed in the app under Sources and licenses.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not yet. It's planned: the same assistant, without the browser, controlling your phone's apps.
