MARIGOLD, TEST BUILD
====================

Marigold is an AI assistant that works on your Mac the way you do: it opens your apps,
clicks and types in them, reads your calendar and mail, browses the web, and tells you what
matters each day. It asks before it does anything consequential, and always asks before
anything involving money.

Because this is a test build (not yet notarized by Apple), macOS needs one extra step:

  1. Drag Marigold into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Terminal (Spotlight: type "Terminal") and paste this line, then press Return:

         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Marigold.app

  3. Open Marigold from Applications. A setup screen walks you through the rest.

What you'll need
  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 or later.
  • An AI: either the setup file someone sent you (Setup → "Got a setup file from someone?"),
    or an API key from an AI provider (the setup screen has a link; about $5 of credit is plenty).
  • Optional but recommended: a free search key from tavily.com (no card needed) pasted into
    Setup → "Web search key". Without it the browser's search results are thin.
  • About 700 MB of space (plus 600 MB if you turn on voice).

Permissions it will ask for (all optional except the first two)
  Accessibility and Screen Recording  – to see and operate your apps
  Full Disk Access                    – only to notice texts that need an answer
  Microphone                          – for dictating (Command-Shift-D) and for talking: click Talk,
                                        then say "Hey Marigold" to start. Until you say it, nothing is kept.
  Notifications                       – for your daily briefings (6 AM, 2 PM, 10 PM)

Things to know
  • The browser has a "Pulse" bar along the bottom (stocks, scores, weather, headlines). The gear
    on it chooses what it tracks; the X hides it and "Show Pulse" brings it back.
  • Everything is stored on your Mac. The only things that leave it are the requests sent
    to the AI provider you choose, and Google's own APIs if you connect a Google account.
  • To stop everything at any time: click the Marigold flower in the menu bar, then "Stop all tasks".
  • Settings has a "Safety & permissions" screen: it shows every action Marigold took and lets
    you revoke anything it remembered.

Found a bug or something confusing? Tell whoever sent you this, and include what you asked
Marigold to do and what happened.
